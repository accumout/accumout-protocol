# Accumout whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Software is moving from people clicking buy buttons to wallets and agents settling bills on their own. Billing rails built around card files and manual approvals do not fit that world.

Accumout exists to make a connected wallet a first-class payment method: one that can renew a plan, meter what gets used and carry a spending budget for an autonomous agent, with settlement on Solana and funds staying in their owner's hands.

Our mission is to give builders a billing layer they can add in an afternoon and trust for years, so pricing can change without rewiring checkout.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1, Foundation.** Public site, live market board, brand and documentation baseline, first conversations with builders.

**Phase 2, Core billing.** Signed authorization, recurring plans from a wallet, hosted pay links, and the access rules that tie plan state to product features.

**Phase 3, Agents and metering.** Spending ceilings for agents, per-call usage billing, webhooks and a public API for integrators.

**Phase 4, Ecosystem.** Drop-in pricing, checkout and status modules, partner integrations, and independent security review of the settlement contracts.
